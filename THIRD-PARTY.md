# Third-party content

The repository's own work is MIT, see [LICENSE](LICENSE): the pack scripts,
`make_pck.mjs`, and the `warring_lords`, `shiny_orbits` and `hello_godot`
examples.

Two examples are vendored from upstream projects and keep their own terms.
Everything here is freely redistributable, but one asset set carries an
attribution requirement, so it is spelled out rather than assumed.
